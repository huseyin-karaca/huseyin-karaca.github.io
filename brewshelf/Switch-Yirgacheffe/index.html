⏱️ Step-by-Step Method

Time Target Weight State Action
00:00 120g 🟢 Switch OPEN Pour gently up to 120g. Avoid heavy agitation.
00:45 240g 🔴 Switch CLOSED Close switch. Pour gently up to 240g. Pouring may take up to 30 seconds.
00:45 - 02:00 240g 🔴 Switch CLOSED Steep for 1 minute and 15 seconds.
02:00 240g 🟢 Switch OPEN Open switch and let it drain completely.

📝 Tasting Notes & Adjustments

Notes: One of the sweetest cups I have ever made. Adjustments for Next Time: In my second attempt, being too gentle resulted in some bitterness. I believe slightly increasing the agitation during the first pour (00:00 - 00:45) would yield better results. Source/Inspiration: Coffee Chronicle (YouTube)

☕️ About the bean (details from the website)

Ethiopia Yirgacheffe is considered one of the most prestigious and distinctive coffees in the world. Grown in the high-altitude Yirgacheffe region in southern Ethiopia, these special beans stand out with their elegant aroma, clean cup, and pronounced fruity notes.

Thanks to its vibrant yet mild acidity, it offers a refreshing drinking experience. With its medium body and long, sweet finish, it is particularly ideal for filter coffee, V60, Chemex, and French Press brewing methods; when roasted appropriately, it can also be thoroughly enjoyed as an espresso.

Carefully selected and meticulously roasted, Ethiopia Yirgacheffe beans reflect the natural character of the region and the purest form of the coffee in every cup. It is an excellent choice for those seeking a sophisticated and aromatic coffee experience that can be enjoyed at any time of the day.

I think today's artificial intelligence jump became possible only after we could effectively learn and process textual data. Text is the medium in which we store almost all our (as humankind) intellectual savings. It is a well-known fact. 

As an over-thinker, I noticed that it is really hard to regularize any thought without writing it down. You need deep, sharp, and patient people, who are willing to discuss for long hours with you otherwise, which is something not easy to find. By writing my thoughts down and pushing myself to publish them on this blog, I at least make use of my inner-validation tools (plus LLMs) effectively and produce (hopefully) good enough thoughts. I will also try to update posts on this blog if I change my thoughts. It would be great if you provided feedback, in any form! 

As can also be understood from the title, if we draw parallels from machine learning, your feedback will serve as a gradient boosting machine, correcting even my minimal mistakes, reducing variance. Some sort of an ensemble learning!
